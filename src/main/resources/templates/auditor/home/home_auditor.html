    A national step toward financial inclusion, transparency, and modernization, Accelerating transactions to fuel investment, trade, and national development,Connecting banks to boost trust, reduce costs, and drive economic performance,Making the financial system faster, smarter, and more accessible for all.

    Future Services and Vision

    Looking ahead, the APA/BPDZ platform will not only offer faster, lower-cost, and ISO 20022-compliant payment services, but will also act as a catalyst for financial inclusion and renewed trust in the banking sector. We will actively promote these services to encourage clients to rely more on banks, shifting away from cash-based habits. With increased usage, the liquidity generated within the platform can be strategically reinvested—allowing the central bank to reduce its dependency on government funding and move toward self-sustainability. This financial independence will, in turn, fuel continuous innovation and new services on the platform. Participating banks will also benefit significantly, gaining access to new revenue streams and a growing, more engaged customer base.
